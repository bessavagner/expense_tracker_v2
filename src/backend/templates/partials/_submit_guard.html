{% comment %} Stops a plain HTML form being submitted twice, and tells the user why. htmx already drops a second request while one is in flight, so the app's 18 htmx forms never needed this. The plain ones did: the importer's four steps and the login form are ordinary browser POSTs, and a double-tap on a slow one sends two. On the importer that used to replay the whole CSV — see Finding 5 in .superpowers/sdd/HANDOFF-login-and-double-submit.md. This is the "I heard you" half of the fix and it is not the guarantee: a reload or a second tab bypasses anything client-side. The server-side guarantee lives in ImportBatch. What this buys is that the round trip stops *looking* broken, which is why the second tap happened at all. Opt out with data-no-submit-guard on the form. Give a button data-pending="…" to have its label swapped while the request is out. {% endcomment %}
